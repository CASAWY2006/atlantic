Discover how to join Atlantic, one of the largest Moroccan GTA RP servers, step by step.

Start your adventure

Step 1: Essential Requirements

Install Steam or Epic Games

To start your GTA RP adventure, you first need a gaming platform. Install Steam or Epic Games on your computer.

Purchase GTA V

GTA V is the base game required to play GTA RP. Make sure you have purchased and installed it via Steam or Epic Games.

Age Requirement: 22 Years

Our Atlantic server has a strict age requirement: you must be at least 22 years old to join us.

Step 2: Preparing for Roleplay

Install FiveM

FiveM is the multiplayer mod that allows you to play GTA RP. Download and install FiveM from its official website.

Join our Discord server

Once FiveM is installed, join the Atlantic Discord server. This is where all communications and onboarding processes take place.

Voice Changer Prohibited

To maintain an authentic roleplay experience, the use of voice changers is strictly prohibited on our server.

Step 3: The Whitelist Process

The Whitelist Interview

After joining our Discord, you will need to complete a "whitelist interview." This is a discussion to assess your understanding of roleplay and the server rules.

Whitelist on our Server

If you pass the interview, you will be added to the whitelist on our server. This will give you access to the game and the Atlantic community.

Step 4: Integrity and Immersion

Anti-Cheat Verification

We perform a quick check to ensure that players are not using cheats. Game integrity is essential for a fair and immersive experience.

Atlantic: The Moroccan GTA RP

Atlantic is proud to be one of the largest Moroccan GTA RP servers, offering a unique experience and a dynamic community. Join us for total immersion in Moroccan roleplay.
