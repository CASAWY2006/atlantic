Règles du Serveur

Server Rules

To ensure the best gaming experience for everyone, here are the essential rules of Atlantic RP.

Comportement

  • Respect all players and staff.
  • No harassment or discrimination.
  • Use appropriate language.
  • Keep roleplay realistic.

Gameplay

  • No cheating or unauthorized modding.
  • Respect roleplay zones.
  • Follow ongoing scenarios.
  • No random killing (RDM).

Communication

  • Use voice chat appropriately.
  • No spamming in chat.
  • Stay in character (IC).
  • Use OOC sparingly.

Sanctions

  • Warning: 1st offense
  • Temporary kick: 2nd offense
  • 24h ban: 3rd offense
  • Permanent ban: Serious offenses
