🎓 Beginner's RP Guide – Atlantic RP

👋 Welcome to Atlantic RP

You've just arrived on an RP server, and you don't know where to start? Don't panic! Here's everything you need to know to play correctly, respect the rules, and fully enjoy the Atlantic RP experience.

1. ⚙️ What is RP?

RP (RolePlay) comes from "Role Play" = playing a role. In Atlantic RP, you embody a fictional character in a virtual city. You live their life, you talk, you react, and you make decisions as if you were really them.

  • ✅ You do not play yourself
  • ❌ You never say "I'll be right back, I have bugs" or "I'm crashing IRL" in-game

2. 👤 Creating your character

Before playing, imagine a story for your character:

  • His name, surname
  • His age
  • His past (poor, rich, former military, reformed thief, etc.)
  • His personality (calm, nervous, talkative, discreet…)

Important: no unrealistic nicknames. Use a realistic first and last name.

3. 🧠 Basic rules to follow

CodeDescription
🔴 No Fear RPBe afraid of dying like IRL.
🔴 No Fail RPNo impossible or unrealistic actions.
🔴 No Power GamingNo abuse of mechanics to win.
🔴 No Meta GamingNo use of out-of-character information.
🔴 No Free KillNo murder without a clear RP reason.

4. 💬 How to talk in RP?

In-game, everything you say is experienced by your character.

  • /me → Character action: /me looks around
  • /do → Description: /do A strong smell of cannabis
  • /twt → Post an RP tweet
  • /911 → Call the police
  • /ems → Call for help

❗ Never speak OOC in voice. Use /ooc if needed.

5. 🚔 Jobs and opportunities

Atlantic RP offers a rich universe:

  • 👮‍♂️ Police
  • 🚑 EMS
  • 🔧 Mechanic
  • 🍔 Restaurateur
  • 🚚 Driver
  • 👤 Gang / Mafia
  • 💼 Entrepreneur

6. 🔒 A semi-whitelisted server

  • ✅ Active anti-cheat
  • ✅ Constant monitoring
  • ✅ Serious community

7. ✨ Tips for getting started

  • Observe veteran players
  • Avoid provocations or insults
  • Speak naturally
  • Develop your character logically
  • Ask questions if you're lost

8. 📌 Sanctions

  • ⚠️ Warning
  • ⛔ Kick or temporary ban
  • 🚫 Permanent ban if rules are not followed

🎉 Conclusion

Atlantic RP is not just a game. It's a complete RP experience. We ask for maturity, creativity, and respect towards other players.

Are you ready? Create your story and make it live. Welcome to Atlantic RP.
